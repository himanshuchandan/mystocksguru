IMPORTANT: What AI Should Actually Do
Do NOT ask AI:
“Which stock should I buy?”
That gives mediocre results.
Instead, feed:
structured market data
news summaries
technical indicators
portfolio context
Then ask:
“Analyze opportunities”
“Rank risk-adjusted setups”
“Suggest stop losses”
“Explain rationale”
LLMs are strongest at:
synthesis
reasoning
contextual explanation
NOT raw market prediction.
